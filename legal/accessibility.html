Accessibility

Last updated October 15, 2026

__ENTITY__ wants everyone to be able to read this website and request a demo, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard alone, or a magnified screen.

What we aim for

The website is built to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. That is the standard we design and test against.

How the website is built

  • Plain HTML with proper headings, so a screen reader can move through each page by its structure.
  • Every form field has a visible label, and required fields are announced when they are used.
  • Everything that can be clicked can also be reached and used with the keyboard, and the keyboard focus is always visible.
  • Text and background colors meet the contrast levels the guidelines require.
  • Pictures of the software carry text descriptions of what they show.
  • Text can be enlarged in the browser without losing content, and the pages work on a phone.
  • Nothing moves, flashes or plays on its own, and the small animations are turned off for anyone whose system asks for reduced motion.

Known limits

The product pictures are images of the software, so their text descriptions summarize what is on screen rather than reproducing it. If you need the content of a picture, write to us and we will describe it.
